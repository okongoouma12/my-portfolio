How much does a website cost in Kenya?

Website prices in Kenya depend on how many pages you need and what the site has to do. A simple landing page typically costs KSh 15,000–30,000. A business website with 4–12 pages runs about KSh 30,000–80,000, and an online store with M-Pesa payments can cost KSh 60,000–200,000 or more.

Remember the running costs too: a domain name is renewed yearly, and hosting setup usually costs KSh 5,000–20,000 per year. Ask any developer to list these separately so nothing is hidden.
