Choosing a domain name: .co.ke vs .com

.co.ke domains signal a local Kenyan business and can help with local search, while .com is more globally recognised and often easier for customers to remember correctly. Availability matters too, since your exact business name may already be taken on one extension.

If you serve mostly local customers in Nairobi or Kisumu, .co.ke is a solid default. If you plan to sell beyond Kenya, .com is usually worth securing as well.
