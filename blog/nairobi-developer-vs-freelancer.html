Website developer in Nairobi: what to look for

A low price can hide slow delivery, poor communication, or a site built on a platform you can't easily edit later. Before hiring a website developer in Nairobi, ask three things: can I see live examples of their work, do I own the domain and code when the project ends, and what happens after launch if something breaks.

A developer who answers these clearly, and offers ongoing support like WhatsApp help and maintenance, is usually a safer long-term choice than the lowest quote.
