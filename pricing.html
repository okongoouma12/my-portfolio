Frequently asked questions


How much does a website cost?

Starter sites begin at KSh 15,000 and business sites run KSh 30,000–80,000. The final price depends on pages, features and content.

How long does it take?

Most business websites take 2–4 weeks once I have your content. Larger systems take longer and get a timeline in the quote.

Do I pay for hosting and a domain?

Yes, these are separate. A .co.ke or .com domain plus hosting setup costs are quoted openly, with no hidden markup.

How do payments work?

Usually a deposit to start, then a balance on launch, paid by M-Pesa or bank. We agree the split before work begins.

What does maintenance include?

Two years of free fixes, updates and support after launch. Extra features or redesigns are quoted separately.

Can customers pay me by M-Pesa on my site?

Yes. I can add M-Pesa payments to online stores and WhatsApp ordering to any site.
