Why every Nairobi business needs a website

Before buying, most people search on their phones. If your business doesn't appear, they will find a competitor who does. A website shows what you sell, where you are and how to reach you, at any hour.

A good site also makes contact easy. A WhatsApp button lets customers message you in one tap, and a Google Maps link helps them find your shop. Add search-friendly pages for "your service + Nairobi" or "+ Kisumu" and new customers start coming to you.
